Our Story

Small town coffee, made by people you know.

Louisiana Bean started with a simple idea: our town deserved a good cup of coffee and a friendly place to drink it. What began as one espresso machine grew into a shop with its doors open every morning and a truck that goes wherever folks need it.

We know most of our regulars by name and by order. Come in once and we'll probably know yours too.

See y'all in the morning.
